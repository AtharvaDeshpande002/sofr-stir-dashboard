Two legs, 6M and 12M calendar spreads only. The ratio is set so the package does not move with the rate level: each leg's daily beta to the average rate of the package's contracts is measured over the window, the hedge ratio is β1 / β2, and it is rounded to the nearest lot ratio up to 4:4. Packages are always panel 1 − k × panel 2, with panel 2 anchored 1–4 contracts after panel 1. 1:1 pairs that are just the standard 6M / 12M fly are left out. ρ on each card is what correlation to the rate level is left after rounding the ratio. Range-bound rules are the same as the list (half-life 1.5–10, range ≥ 2.5bp and ≥ 4 normal days, fades ≥ 70%, |ρ| < 0.25). Star a card to keep it in ★ FAVOURITES; range-bound ones at an edge also show in ALERTS.

RESIDUAL HISTORY · click a row
The curve of 3M spreads (16 points from the front) is fitted each day on its three main patterns, learned from the fit window: F1 overall steepness (how much hiking is priced, mostly the front), F2 front vs back steepness, F3 a bend. 3M spreads already exclude the outright rate level, so a parallel rate move doesn't touch any of this. What the model can't explain is the residual. A structure's residual = its value minus the model's fair value; SELL when it's rich (residual high vs its own history, z ≥ +), BUY when cheap. Because the common moves are taken out, these residuals mean-revert more cleanly than raw levels and the structures carry little level/slope risk (see the exposure columns). Fitted on generic positions (constant distance from the front); in-sample, so treat the z as a ranking, not a probability.
